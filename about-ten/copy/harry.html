hi, i'm harry.

co-founder, rep enforcer, the rat who never rounds up.

i'm building the gym brand you've been guessing about. a small box on every machine that counts every rep, every set, every rest. no notes app. no "about ten."

because a guess is not progress. a number is. about ten. exists to say that part out loud.

be like me. join the body count club™ & have the most honest workout of ur life.

why i built this.

i didn't plan to start a fitness company. i planned to finish my set. but the gym floor was loud, vague and out of touch. big promises. "about ten." so i did what rats do best. i counted.

what started it all.

a guy on the leg press said twelve. it was nine. i've lived in gyms my whole life. nobody counts. everybody guesses. and a guess can't tell u if ur getting stronger.

what i believe.

reps are proof, not vibes. lifters don't need more motivation, they need a number. fitness doesn't have to scream to be taken seriously. he's stronger than u because he counted. count like the rat.
