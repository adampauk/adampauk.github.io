the rest period / how-to

how to rest without scrolling (on purpose)

by harry
stand-in photo

Your two minutes took eleven. Not because you're lazy. Because the phone was in your hand.

1. phone in your pocket.

The box counts the sets, so you don't need the phone to keep track.

2. give the rest a job.

Water, a stretch for the muscle you just worked, the pin in for the next set.

3. let something time it.

The box times the rest between sets, so you can see whether two minutes was two minutes.

the bottom line

Rest is part of the workout. Scrolling is part of a different one.
