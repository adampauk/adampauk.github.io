before u count:

Does it round up?
No. That was you. The box counts the weight stack moving: up and down is one rep.
Does it work on every machine?
Every machine with a weight stack. It sits on the stack, so barbells and dumbbells are a no.
Do I need to touch my phone?
Once, to tap in. Then it goes in your pocket and the box does the counting.
What if it miscounts?
It happens. Tell it with one tap. It takes it better than most people at the gym.
