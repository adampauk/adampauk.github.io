Value proposition

The machine counts, so members just train and owners see each machine’s use.

For members, free
  • Sets and reps recorded with no typing
  • The weight in one tap
  • The rest timed by itself, also on a locked phone
For the gym owner, paid
  • How often each machine is used, every week
  • Buy, move or retire machines by use, not by feel
  • Logging as a service for every member
Idea generation: one of ten ideas in our COCD box. It won once we learned that the gym, not the member, would pay.
