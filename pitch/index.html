BENNETT · TEST MATRIX

Three learnings, and what we changed.

01
Some members like it, but the owner must buy it.
→
The owner is our only buyer: independent gyms with about 15–50 strength machines. Members use it and pay nothing.
02
Data alone does not sell.
→
Decisions, not data. In the trial the owner names one machine decision first: buy, move, service or retire. The report leads with change (proposed).
03
Owners want results before they pay, with little risk and staff work.
→
No paid check: a free four-week trial with full use; the owner signs only at the read-out. We install, remove and fix.
+
About ourselves: our questions sometimes led the answer.
→
New interview rules: consent on tape, neutral questions before we show anything, the offer only at the end.

BRIJAN · 4P

The 4Ps: one offer, from the Brand Key.

Product
What do we offer?INSIGHT 4 · BENEFITS 5 · DISCRIMINATOR 8
One sensor per strength machine (retrofit, no new machines), a member app (iPhone first) and a weekly usage report for the owner. We install, support and review it once a year. Left out on purpose: screens, cameras, coaching, leaderboards.
Price
What does the owner pay?TARGET 3 · VALUES 6 · REASONS 7
Test prices: €249 per machine once + €20 per machine a month, 12 months, at least 10 machines (20 machines: €4,980 once + €400 a month). A free four-week trial with full use comes first; the owner signs at the read-out. Members pay nothing.
Place
Where and how does the owner buy?ROOTS 1 · TARGET 3 · VALUES 6
Direct, in person: walk-in → floor walk → free trial → read-out. The Netherlands until Jan 2027; from Feb 2027 around Heilbronn. We install and remove ourselves. Chains after ≥ 10 paying gyms and 3 references.
Promotion
How does the owner hear of us and say yes?INSIGHT 4 · VALUES 6 · REASONS 7 · ESSENCE 9
Open with the owner's own gap; the priced offer only at the end. A sample weekly report (labelled sample), not the device. “Every set seen” · “No new machines, no screens, no camera”. Referrals at every read-out.
One thread: we add, we don't replace, and we observe.
