Three learnings, and what we changed.

What we learned
What we changed
Some members like it, but the owner must buy it.
The owner is our only buyer: independent gyms with about 15–50 strength machines. Members use it and pay nothing.
Data alone does not sell.
Decisions, not data. In the trial the owner names one machine decision first; the report leads with what changed (proposed).
Owners want results before they pay, with little staff work.
A free four-week trial with full use; the owner signs only at the read-out. We install, remove and fix.
Our questions sometimes led the answer.
New interview rules: consent on tape, neutral questions before we show anything, the offer only at the end.

One offer, from the Brand Key.

Product
What do we offer?
From insight, benefits, discriminator
One sensor per strength machine (retrofit, no new machines), a member app (iPhone first) and a weekly usage report for the owner. We install, support and review it once a year. Left out on purpose: screens, cameras, coaching, leaderboards.
Price
What does the owner pay?
From target, values, reasons to believe
Test prices: €249 per machine once + €20 per machine a month, 12 months, at least 10 machines (20 machines: €4,980 once + €400 a month). A free four-week trial with full use comes first. Members pay nothing.
Place
Where does the owner buy?
From root strengths, target, values
Direct, in person: gym visit, floor walk, free trial, order at the end. The Netherlands until Jan 2027; from Feb 2027 around Heilbronn. Chains after proof.
Promotion
How does the owner say yes?
From insight, values, reasons, essence
Open with the owner's own gap; the priced offer only at the end. A sample weekly report (labelled sample), events and fairs, referrals; calls and e-mails within the legal rules, no letters.
One thread: we add, we don't replace, and we observe.

Two trends, four futures.

Our idea in every one: members' training counted, each machine's use shown to the owner, on the machines a gym already owns.

1 · Threat

Smart chains

Head offices buy connected floors; our pool of ordinary machines shrinks.
Be the data for the rest of the floor; as an option, the technology others build in.
2 · Later opportunity

Lean chains

Chains run ordinary machines longer. Big accounts, slow decisions.
The chain path after 10 paying gyms and 3 references. An upside, not in our numbers.
3 · Likely near term

Mixed floors

Owners add a connected circuit and keep the rest: 12 of 23 gyms in Heilbronn-Franken already do.
“Bring the rest of your floor into the same data.”
4 · Best fit

The owner's floor

Independents keep their machines longer and compete on service.
The plan as built: walk-ins, the free four-week trial, referrals.
We prepare for 3 and 4 for the next two to three years (our reading, not a forecast) and watch for very cheap connected machines. Deloitte, 2026: chain facilities +12.6 %, independents +2.4 % (Germany, 2025). Playlist & EGYM, 2026: more than 33,000 locations.
