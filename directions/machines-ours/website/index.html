readout proto

smaller, smarter and zero new machines.

built from years of watching gym floors. a small sensor that clicks onto the weight stack of the machines a gym already owns, and turns each one into a machine that counts. no new equipment. no cables. no rebuild. here’s what the machines learn:

  • counts every rep
  • counts every set
  • times every rest
  • knows which machine it is on
  • knows who is lifting, with one tap
  • saves every set
  • scores every workout from 0 to 100
  • says what made the score
  • plans the next weight
  • busy hours for the owner
  • the busiest machine
  • the quietest machine
  • new members still training in week four
  • members who have gone quiet
  • a live set for the trainer
  • the next plan, sent to the phone
  • the gym total for the tv
  • low battery warnings
  • coin cell power
  • bluetooth low energy
  • no cables
  • no camera
  • no video
  • no new machines
  • clicks onto a holder
  • works on leg press, chest press, lat pulldown, seated row, leg curl and cable stacks
  • names only with a yes
  • private by default

counted, timed, remembered.

think of readout as the training log the machine always should have kept. it counts the set while you lift, starts the rest the moment you stop, and keeps every set for the day you want to look back.

the live set, as the sensor sees it. seven of ten reps, set two of three. the rest starts on its own when the set ends.

the week, as a grid. every coloured step is a workout on a machine. sample week.

if it feels simple. it is.

we didn’t want to change the machines, because the machines are fine. the sensor clicks onto a holder on the weight stack and stays there, set after set.

on the stack. a holder keeps it in place. the coin cell keeps it going.

the back is a holder and a coin cell.

one port on the side, nothing to plug in on the gym floor. a standard cr2477 coin cell does the rest, and the owner sees when one is running low.
