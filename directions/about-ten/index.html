why it exists

the story

ask anyone how many they did. “about ten.”

nobody counts. we guess, and a guess can’t tell you if you’re getting stronger.

so we put a small box on every machine. it counts every rep, every set, every rest.

you said about ten. it was 7.

the truthnobody counts at the gym. we guess reps, rest and weights, so nobody knows if they’re getting stronger.
the nameabout ten. what you say when you guess. every time someone says it at the gym, they say our name.
the boxsits on the weight stack and counts. it never talks, it only shows numbers.
harrythe rat who never rounds up. harry talks, the box counts.
the jokealways the same one: what you said, and what the box counted.
the voiceall in: bold, funny, a little provocative, never crude, never about bodies or beginners.

hi, i'm harry.

co-founder, head of counting, the rat who never rounds up.

i've lived in gyms my whole life. under the bench press, mostly. i've heard everything. "about ten." "last set." "two minutes." none of it was true.

so we built a box that counts. you lift, it counts, the app tells you what to do next time. i supervise.
