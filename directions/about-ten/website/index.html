the box

a box on every machine.

it sits on the weight stack and counts every rep, set and rest by itself. no screen. no buttons. nothing to press.

2magnetsit goes on the stack in seconds. no screws, no tools.
0taps during a setyour phone stays in your pocket.
1coin cella CR2477, inside the lid.

how it works

three steps. one of them is lifting.

01tap in.hold your phone to the tag on the machine. once. then it goes back in your pocket.
02lift.the box feels the stack move. one rise and fall is one rep. the rest clock starts when the stack lands.
03look.every set, last time next to it, and what to do next. after the set or after the workout.

what it counts

every set, measured.

reps and tempo8 of 10leg press, 85 kg

each rep is one rise and fall of the stack. the last ones get slower, and the box sees that too.

rest1:12your plan says 2:00

the clock starts when the stack lands and stops when you lift again. no stopwatch.

when to go up85 kg10, 10, 10, twice in a row

every set at the top of your range, twice in a row. then the smallest step up.

workout score95out of 100

one number for every workout, with what made it and what would raise it.

numbers in the pictures are samples until the pilot runs.

is it right?

every count, checked by hand.

we film every set on the test machines. two people count the reps on the video without seeing what the box said. then the two counts go side by side, and every miss is kept.

when it misses, we say so. harry insisted.
