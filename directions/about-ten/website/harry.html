hi, i’m harry.

co-founder, head of counting, the rat who never loses count.

i’ve lived in gyms my whole life. under the bench press, mostly. i’ve watched a million people lose count at six. one, two, three, four, five, six… eight? nine? ten. “about ten.”

so we built a box that remembers. you lift, it counts, the app tells you what you did last time and what to do now. i supervise.

counting in your head is dead. so is the plateau.

why we built it.

everyone loses count. then they say about ten. then the mirror says no progress and they quit. the numbers would have said yes. someone had to remember. i volunteered.

what started it.

a guy on the leg press said twelve. it was nine. he wasn’t lying. he lost count at six, like everybody. that’s when i knew.

what i believe.

a number beats motivation. the mirror lies. and nobody, nobody, does ten.
