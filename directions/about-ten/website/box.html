before you count

does it round up?
No. That was you. It counts the weight stack moving: up and down is one rep.
does it work on every machine?
Every machine with a weight stack: leg press, chest press, lat pulldown and the like. Barbells and dumbbells come later. They’re not ready.
do i need to touch my phone?
Once, to tap in. Then back in your pocket. Your phone has done enough to your rest times.
what if it miscounts?
Correct the set with one tap, and the fix is saved with the workout. It takes feedback better than most people at the gym.
what powers it?
A CR2477 coin cell, inside the lid. How many months it lasts gets measured in the pilot, and the number goes here.
