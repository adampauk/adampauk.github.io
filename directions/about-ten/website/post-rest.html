between sets / how-to

how to rest without scrolling (you won't)

by harry

Two minutes is two minutes. With a phone in your hand it's eleven, and one of them was an album.

1. phone in your pocket.

The box counts the sets. Your phone can sit this one out.

2. give the rest a job.

Water. A stretch for the muscle you just used. The pin in for the next set. Look busy.

3. let something time it.

The box times every rest, so the report shows whether two minutes was two minutes. Spoiler: it wasn't.

the bottom line

Rest is part of the workout. Scrolling is a different workout. Your thumbs are already strong.
