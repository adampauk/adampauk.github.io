about the box

harry, does it round up?
No. That was you. I count the stack moving: up and down is one rep.
does it see my form?

about rep amnesia

what is rep amnesia?
Losing count mid-set, usually around six. It isn’t you: counting while lifting is two jobs, and your brain picked the heavy one.
is it contagious?
Only in group classes.
is there a cure?
A box on the machine. It remembers, so you don’t have to.
why “about ten”?
It’s what rep amnesia makes you say. You lost count, so it was about ten. It was 7.
is harry real?
Very. He has never lost count. Not once.
