Direction 1, ours, built on the Spotify copy

The data is the joke.

Real numbers from real gyms are funnier than anything we could write.

Round two: our version, made with the spotify-design skill from the copy. Kept: the black ground, the bold titles, the white pills, the shelves and the number cards. Made ours: the mark, the accent colour and the covers.

The story

Nobody has ever counted the gym. We do, and it’s hilarious.

Gyms are full of strange, very human habits. Monday is chest day for everybody. Friday is nobody’s leg day. Rests get longer when a phone comes out. Nobody has ever counted any of it.

A small sensor on every machine counts it all, so the brand never has to invent a joke or a claim. The product stays exact and calm. The posts simply publish the numbers.

The enemy

Fitness marketing that makes things up.

The promise

Every number is true. Every joke is gym-wide, never about one person.
