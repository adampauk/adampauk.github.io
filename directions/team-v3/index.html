Brand direction, round three

SAME GYM

Lift alone. Count together.

Everyone at the gym trains alone. The sensor counts every rep on every machine, so for the first time the whole gym has one number to lift together, and people stay for each other.

Open the websiteOpen the appSee the posts

The idea

Turn a room full of strangers into a team.

What people feel

Headphones in, nobody knows anybody. You can train at the same gym for a year and never say hello.

What the sensor makes possible

It counts every rep on every machine, so the gym finally has shared numbers: the week’s total, a monthly challenge, one gym against another.

The promise

Your workout stays yours. Every rep also counts for the gym, and somebody notices.

Who it’s for

Members of mid-sized gyms who train alone, and owners who want people to stay for the people.

Why it could win

40% less likely to cancel. Members who had made a friend at the gym in the previous three months were 40% less likely to cancel over the next seven months.TRP 10,000 study, Hillsdon and Bedford, Health Club Management

The risk

Leaderboards can scare beginners. So they count showing up, never kilos, and names appear only for people who said yes.

The name

SAME GYM

same gym is what people say when they find out they train at the same place: instant common ground. Two plain words that carry the whole idea, everyone at your gym is on one team.
