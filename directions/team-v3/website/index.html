How it works

Three steps. Then just lift.

  1. 1Clip it on

    The gym clips a sensor onto each machine’s weight stack. Nothing else changes.

  2. 2Tap in, then lift

    Tap your phone on the sensor once. Then just lift. Every rep is counted.

  3. 3Add it up

    Your set lands in the app and in the gym’s total, the moment you finish.

The sensor

Small. On the stack. Counts everything.

One sensor sits on the weight stack of each machine. When the stack moves, it counts. That is the whole trick, and it works on the machines your gym already has.

  • On the weight stackIt moves when the stack moves, so every rep is counted.
  • Nothing to typeNo buttons on the machine. No logging between sets.
  • No cameraIt measures the stack, never people. No video, ever.
  • Made on a gym floorBuilt and tested at our first gym in Brno.

Questions

Good questions.

Do I need my phone during the workout?

Only to tap in once. The sensor counts every rep, so there is nothing to type between sets.

Will other members see my name?

Only if you choose. By default your reps count toward the gym’s total without your name.

Do the strongest people always win?

No. Leaderboards count sessions and consistency. A beginner who shows up three times a week ranks above a strong lifter who comes once.

Which machines does it work on?

Machines with a weight stack: leg press, chest press, lat pulldown, cable machines. Free weights are not counted yet.

Does it film anyone?

No. There is no camera. The sensor measures the movement of the weight stack.
