How he is made

  • A real rat’s buildSmall head, long snout, beady eyes on the sides, long pink feet and tail, a bit of a belly. Grey, with silver tips.
  • The cap and the headphonesA washed navy cap with stitched panels, orange over-ear headphones. The orange is the brand.
  • The sensor’s studioThe same renderer that made the sensor pictures: real light, soft shadows, depth. The fur is dozens of layers of single strands.
  • Made hereAbout 5 seconds a picture on this Mac, so a new pose or moment takes minutes.
