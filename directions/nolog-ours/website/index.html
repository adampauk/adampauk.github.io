How it works

Tap your phone to the sensor, and the machine takes it from there.

1. Choose your plan

Pick today’s workout in the app before you leave home. Then the phone can stay in your bag.

2. Tap the sensor

Start each machine by tapping your phone against the readout sensor on it. Once.

3. Just lift

The sensor counts every rep and the rest starts on its own. Nothing to type, nothing to start, nothing to log.

Why phones and sets don’t mix

In a Hiram College study, texting during exercise cut balance by 45% and lowered workout intensity. We built readout so your phone can stay in the bag.

Questions

Do I need my phone at all?

Once at each machine, to tap in, and after the workout, to see your summary. During your sets, no. Showing the rest on the sensor itself is in the plan.

What does the sensor record?

The movement of the weight stack: reps, sets and rest. No camera and no video.

Does it work on every machine?

On machines with a weight stack. Free weights are not counted yet.
