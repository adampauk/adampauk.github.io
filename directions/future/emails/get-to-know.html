Five things to try in your first week.

One

Tap to start.

Hold your phone near the sensor on the machine. That is the whole setup.

Two

Just lift.

The sensor counts every rep and times every rest. Nothing to type.

Three

Look at your Lock Screen.

Your set shows up live while you lift.

Four

Fill your rings.

Reps, sets and machines. Three rings for every workout.

Five

Read your summary.

Every workout, written down for you. Sample numbers in the pictures.
