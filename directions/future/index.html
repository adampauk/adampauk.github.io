The future of the gym.

Say hello to the smart gym.

Round one: copied as close as possible from Apple, its product page, its Fitness app, its e-mails and its posts, with our sensor and story swapped in. The skills and our own version come next.

See the website ›See the app ›

The story

Phones, cars and homes got smart years ago. The gym, the place you go to get better, still forgets every rep the moment you let go.

readout is a small sensor that clicks onto the machines a gym already has. It counts every rep, times every rest and writes the workout down by itself. Members see their progress. Gyms see their floor. Trainers see who needs them.

Apple launches the future as if it were obvious: one object, one sentence, a lot of white space. This direction does the same for the gym, for normal people, not for lab coats.

The enemy

Machines that forget you.

The promise

Every machine smart, without replacing a single one.

Photography

The object alone, on pure white or pure black, in soft light, with no props. People appear in real moments, never posed with the product.
