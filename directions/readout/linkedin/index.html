04Why a sensor
AP
Adam PaukFounder, readout  now

Every gym app asks members to log their sets. Almost nobody does it for more than two weeks.

So we took the typing out. A sensor on the machine counts the reps, reads the weight and times the rest. The member just lifts. The app shows what happened and what to do next time.

The best data is the data nobody had to enter.

Why a sensor, not another app.Nothing to type between sets.readout

05A data story
AP
Adam PaukFounder, readout  now

We looked at 18,240 counted reps from our own gym. [sample number, swap before posting]

Sets started after about two minutes of rest were stronger than sets started after one: more reps at the same weight. Members who rushed their rest also plateaued sooner.

The app now tells members when their rest is done instead of guessing. Small thing. Measurable difference.
