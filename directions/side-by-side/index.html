The data is the joke

Copied from Spotify. Skill: spotify-design. Open the copy Open ours

Kept from the copy: the black room, the big tight titles, the white pills, the shelves, the colourful number cards, the mini player, the voice.

Made ours:

  1. The mark: a circle with the rep wave, the weight stack moving as the sensor sees it, instead of three bars.
  2. The accent: highlighter yellow, the colour a true number gets marked with, instead of Spotify green. Brand parts only.
  3. The covers: drawn from the set itself, its wave, instead of plain gradient squares.
