Brand directions, round two

Copy and ours, side by side.

Each copied look became a design skill. The skill then made our version from the copy: the craft stays, a few named parts become ours. Left is the faithful copy, right is ours, for every piece: website, app, e-mails, posts, stories and the direction page with its brand kit.

Direction 1

The data is the joke

Copied from Spotify. Skill: spotify-design. Open the copy Open ours

Kept from the copy: the black room, the big tight titles, the white pills, the shelves, the colourful number cards, the mini player, the voice.

Made ours:

  1. The mark: a circle with the rep wave, the weight stack moving as the sensor sees it, instead of three bars.
  2. The accent: highlighter yellow, the colour a true number gets marked with, instead of Spotify green. Brand parts only.
  3. The covers: drawn from the set itself, its wave, instead of plain gradient squares.

Direction 2

Wake up your machines

Copied from Teenage Engineering. Skill: teenage-engineering-design. Open the copy Open ours

Kept from the copy: the pale grey pages, the object lit like an instrument, thin lowercase type, the capitals lists, the stage badge, the black-glass screens, the keys, the step grid.

Made ours:

  1. The colour code: Teenage Engineering’s encoder colours become the gym’s plate colours (red 25 kg, blue 20, yellow 15, green 10, white 5), so every colour on a screen means a weight.
  2. The instrument: the ring dial becomes the readout scale, a ruler with one tick per rep.

Direction 3

Strong for life

Copied from Neko Health. Skill: neko-health-design. Open the copy Open ours

Kept from the copy: the quiet serif and plain sans, the white space, the word-by-word statement, the calm results with status dots, the warm photo tone, every page structure.

Made ours:

  1. The words: Neko’s tagline formula (your health, ahead of time) becomes our own line, Lift for later, and the statement is rewritten in our words.
  2. The ground: the clinic’s cool blue-greys become chalk and stone, the colours of a gym floor. Status colours stay.

Direction 4

Nothing to log

Copied from Brick. Skill: brick-design. Open the copy Open ours

Kept from the copy: the large plain type, the tap as the hero, one big state per screen, the blue button, the black-and-white photos, the three steps, the FAQ.

Made ours:

  1. The colour block: Brick’s forest green becomes graphite, the matte black of the sensor itself, so the counting screen goes dark like the object.
  2. The words: Brick’s take-back-from-your-phone formula becomes our own line, You lift. The gym writes it down.

Direction 5

Lift alone, count together

Copied from Strava. Skill: strava-design. Open the copy Open ours

Kept from the copy: the activity cards, the stat blocks, the sign-up card, heavy numbers over black-and-white photos, the plain voice, every page structure.

Made ours:

  1. The colour: Strava orange becomes 20 kg blue, the plate and the bar everybody in a gym shares.
  2. The name: Strava’s heavy italic capitals become our name, upright and lowercase.
  3. The words: kudos becomes a spot, the gym’s own word for backing someone, and the motivation formula becomes our line, Lift alone, count together.

Direction 7

The future of the gym

Copied from Apple. Skill: apple-launch-design. Open the copy Open ours

Kept from the copy: the white space, one object per screen, the big short headlines, the black Fitness app, the Lock Screen live set, blue buttons and chevron links, the wordplay.

Made ours:

  1. The type: Apple’s own SF Pro, licensed for Apple devices only, becomes Geist, a free face with the same precision.
  2. The rings: the Fitness app’s three rings become the readout scale, three tick rulers for reps, sets and machines, in the same colours.
  3. The words: Apple’s keynote formula (say hello to) becomes our line, Meet the smart gym.
