Brand direction, round three

ziitra

Every machine in your gym, now smart.

Phones, cars and homes got smart. The gym is next: one small sensor turns every machine a gym already has into a smart one, simple enough for everyone.

Open the websiteOpen the appSee the posts

The idea

The future of the gym, on the machines it already has.

What people feel

Everything in life got smart except the gym. It still runs on memory, paper and guesswork.

What the sensor makes possible

Every machine knows when you lift. It counts every rep, times every rest and writes the workout down.

The promise

Your gym becomes a smart gym, and it feels simple from the first tap.

Who it’s for

Everyone who trains, from the first-timer to the regular, and the gym owners who want to be first.

Why it could win

One story for everyone. Members, owners and investors all hear the same thing: the future of the gym, starting in Brno.

The risk

Selling the future must never promise what does not exist yet. So the site keeps a clear list of what works today and what is planned.

The name

ziitra

ziitra is the Czech word zítra, tomorrow, with the long í written as two i’s. The future of the gym, from a company in Brno, in a word nobody else uses.
