What it does

It knows when you lift.

Reps

Every rep, counted.

The sensor feels the weight stack move and counts each rep as it happens.

Rest

Every rest, timed.

When the set ends, the rest starts by itself, and it knows when you are ready again.

Record

Every workout, written down.

Reps, weight and rest for every set, and one score at the end.

The sensor

Small enough to forget. Smart enough to count.

It lives on the weight stack, where every lift begins.

  • Clicks onto the stackOn the machines your gym already has.
  • Counts from the movementWhen the stack moves, it counts. That’s all it needs.
  • No cameraIt measures the stack, never people.
  • Made on a gym floorBuilt and tested at our first gym in Brno.

How it works

Click it on. Tap once. Lift.

  1. 1

    Click it on

  2. 2

    Tap once

  3. 3

    Just lift

    Your workout writes itself, set by set.

The app

Your training, finally written down.

Everything the sensors count lands in one quiet app: what to do next, your set as it happens, one score for the workout, and your gym, machine by machine.

Questions

Questions.

Do I need new machines?

No. The sensor clicks onto the weight stack of the machines your gym already has.

Do I need my phone while I train?

Only to tap in once. After that the sensor counts every rep and the rest starts by itself.

Does it film anyone?

No. There is no camera. The sensor measures the movement of the stack.

What works today?

Counting reps and timing rest on stack machines, written into the app. Everything else on this page that is not there yet is marked as planned.
