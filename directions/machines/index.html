the beauty of upgrading.

readout proto

smaller, smarter and zero new machines.

built from years of watching gym floors. a small sensor that clicks onto the weight stack of the machines you already own, and turns each one into a machine that counts. no new equipment. no cables. no rebuild.

first at fitpods, brno. read the specs
