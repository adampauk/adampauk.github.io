readout

Meet
the smart gym.

A small sensor that clicks onto the machines your gym already has. It counts every rep, times every rest and writes your workout down for you. No typing, no buttons, no guessing. The future of the gym, made for everyone.

First at FitPods in Brno.

Get it for your gym

a smart machine.

One sensor per machine. Leg press, chest press, lat pulldown, every one of them. No new equipment and no cables. The gym you already train in becomes a smart gym.

Lift it.
Logged.

Start your set and just lift. The sensor feels the weight stack move and counts every rep as it happens, so there is nothing to type, tap or remember.

Rest. Ready.
Go.

When the set ends, the rest timer starts on its own. It knows your plan, so it knows when you’re ready for the next set, and how many reps to aim for.

Get by with a little help
from every machine.

Every sensor on the floor works for you. Walk up to any machine, tap once and lift. The machine remembers the rest.

How does it work?

The sensor sits on the weight stack. Each lift and lower is one rep, and each pause is your rest. When the set ends, the whole set lands in your app.

One tap to start.

Tap your phone on the sensor once, and the machine knows it’s you. Then just lift.

Your workout writes itself.

Every set is saved with its reps, weight and rest. At the end you get one score for the whole workout, and what to do next time.

The readout app

One number for
every workout.

Every workout gets a score from 0 to 100, and every score explains itself: progress, effort, rest and consistency, in words anyone can read.

Privacy is built in.

Your workouts belong to you. The sensor has no camera and records no video. Your gym sees how its machines are used, and sees your name only if you say yes.

Beautifully
simple.

Gym staff click a sensor onto a holder and it’s ready. Members tap once and lift. The battery is a standard coin cell, and the gym’s app says when one is running low.

See the app for gyms

No cables. Ever.

A standard coin cell powers the sensor, so it fits any machine, anywhere on the floor.

Stays put.

It clicks onto a holder on the weight stack and stays there, set after set.

Works with what you have.

Leg press, chest press, cable stacks. If a machine has a weight stack, the sensor can count it.

Upgrade, don’t replace.

Good machines don’t need replacing. Making them smart is better for gyms and better for the planet.
