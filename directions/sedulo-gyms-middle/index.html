Sedulo

Lift together.

Gyms buy it because members stay. A sensor on every machine counts every rep, set and rest. Members see their own progress and beat their own bests first. Then the gym joins in: spots, one shared number each week, challenges, gym against gym.

Open the websiteTap through the appCompare all three

The idea in one chain

Counting makes progress visible. Visible progress is worth cheering. Cheered members stay.

It countsA sensor on every weight-stack machine counts every rep, set and rest. Members tap in once and lift.
You see itEvery member sees their own progress: new bests, weights going up, when to go heavier, streaks.
The gym sees itWorkouts land in the gym's feed. Members spot each other, the week has one shared total, challenges run for every level.
Members stayPeople stay where they get stronger and where someone notices. That is what the gym pays for.

Community is stressed more here than in the direction for lifters, but the whole product is told: counting, progress and insights first, the social side on top. Most challenges are against your own last month. Members against members, and gym against gym, come second.

Short and sure, a few big words: set, spot, best, together. Never a borrowed slogan. Numbers are marked as samples until they are real.

Motion

  • Numbers count up once, when first seen.
  • App screens slide like iOS, tabs switch at once.
  • A spot gives a short press and pop.
  • The gym screen breathes: a new best every few seconds.
  • No confetti, no mascot, nothing bouncing.

Picked over black and white and more orange

In the middle.

  • Orange for every main button: book a pilot, the app's buttons, the active tab and tags.
  • The big headlines end in orange: every best, last month, joins in, on the wall, before they cancel, measure it.
  • One whole orange section, the pilot, plus the week's number, with white headings on the orange.
  • The gym screen's total is orange, the posts carry an orange bar, the machine sticker is orange.
  • The rest stays black and white: the other sections, the tiles, the owner view's week card, the e-mail headers.
  • Everything else is the same: Sedulo, Nike's structure and type, the newest sensor renders, the story and product.
  • Picked on 21 November. Black and white and more orange stay online beside it on the comparison page.
  • No mascot. Harry does not fit a brand built on real people.

Sedulo for gyms is a concept in development. The sensor is a prototype, shown as the newest renders from the sensor chat. The app, the gym screen and the owner view are concepts. Every number is a sample unless a source is named.

Sources: half of new members leave within six months, HFA and IHRSA figures cited by Health Club Management, 2024. Members who made a friend at the gym in their first three months were 40% less likely to cancel, TRP 10,000 study, Health Club Management. Owner findings: Justus's fieldwork with 35 gym contacts, October 2026.
