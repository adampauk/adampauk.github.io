How to use this document

This document is Sedulo’s strategic compass for selling to gyms.
It says who we are, why we exist, how we act and whom we serve.
It is the base for all communication, design, content and the product itself.

Use it when you are:

  • writing or designing anything with the Sedulo name on it, for a gym or its members,
  • deciding whether something fits the brand,
  • briefing a team, an agency or a partner.

The brand key is not a rulebook.

It is a working frame that helps the brand grow without losing itself.

This key belongs to the gyms direction: the gym buys, the members lift. Version 1, 21 November 2026. Positioned beside Myzone after reading its own pages that day. Numbers in pictures are samples until the pilot runs.

SeduloBrand Key00

Root Strength | Where Sedulo comes from

Sedulo

Sedulo started on a gym floor in Brno and grew through calls with gyms in the Netherlands and Germany. Two gaps came up: new members are on their own after the first weeks, and members who go quiet drop off the radar. On the weights floor nobody sees the effort, so nobody notices when it stops.

Sedulo answers one question for a gym:

“Who is getting stronger here, and who is about to stop?”

Its roots are three principles:

A real gym floor, not a lab.Effort over ability.Nobody goes quiet unnoticed.

What it is

The ground the brand stands on. Where it came from, and what gives it a right to exist.

What it isn’t

A founding legend or a business plan. It’s the experience the brand grew out of: a gym floor, and what gyms told us in October.

Why it matters

A brand that knows where it comes from keeps its shape when the market around it shifts.

SeduloBrand Key01

Brand Purpose | Why Sedulo exists

Sedulo’s purpose

We help gyms keep their members, by making every member’s effort on the weights floor visible.

We turn effort nobody sees into a reason to stay.

Most members will never top a leaderboard, and most of their effort leaves no trace. The extra plate, the first full set, the sixth week in a row: nobody sees it. So a good start fades quietly, and the gym hears about it when the cancellation comes in.

Sedulo makes that effort visible. The machines count every set, every member sees their own progress first, and the gym can cheer it, and notice when someone goes quiet.

For the member, a reason to come back. For the gym, members who stay.

What it is

Why Sedulo exists beyond selling sensors to gyms. The change it wants to make on the weights floor.

What it isn’t

A product description or a sales target.

Why it matters

It decides what we build next, and what we say no to. Anything that doesn’t help a member stay is a no.

SeduloBrand Key02

Competitive Environment | Where it stands

Where Sedulo stands

Gyms already pay to keep members motivated. Myzone made it a category, in 5,000+ facilities by its own count. What decides is what each option can see.

  • Heart-rate systems (Myzone) – effort points for cardio and classes, from a belt or a watch.
  • Smart circuits (EGYM, milon) – they track well, on their own machines, which the gym buys or leases.
  • The gym’s own app – visits and bookings, nothing from the floor.
  • Paper and logging apps – they work while a member types every set.
  • Trainers – the best motivation there is, and there are never enough of them.
Sedulo is motivation technology for the weights floor: every set seen, on the machines a gym already owns, with nothing to wear.

SeduloBrand Key03

Target Audience | Who it’s for

Sedulo is for

Two people meet at the machine: the gym that buys Sedulo, and the member who lifts.

  • Owners and managers of staffed, independent gyms, often one site, with a floor of weight-stack machines and someone on the floor.
  • Gyms that keep members by caring for them, and want the weights floor to show it.
  • Members who lift, from the first week to the tenth year. Beginners most of all: the first 30 days decide.
  • Trainers who want to see their members between sessions.
For gyms that keep members by caring for them.

Not yet: unstaffed 24/7 sites, groups and chains. 0 of 16 said yes to our mails in October.

What it is

Who Sedulo is for right now: the gym that buys, and the members and trainers who use it.

What it isn’t

Every gym. Unstaffed and chain sites may come later. Today they don’t answer, and they have no one on the floor to act on what Sedulo shows.

Why it matters

The gyms most likely to say yes are the ones who already care. A brand that talks to them is heard. One that talks to every gym is heard by none.

SeduloBrand Key04

Consumer Insight | What people feel

The insight

Members join to change something. In the first weeks, nothing seems to change, and nobody seems to notice.

The work on the machines leaves no trace. The mirror moves slowly, the effort is invisible to everyone else, and a quiet week turns into a quiet month. Half of new members leave within six months.

Members who made a friend in their first three months were 40 % less likely to cancel. People stay where someone notices.

Sources: HFA and IHRSA figures, and the TRP 10,000 study, both cited by Health Club Management.

“

Nobody would notice if I stopped coming.

”

The gym’s side, from a manager in October: members who stop coming drop off the radar, and nothing tells the trainers whom to call.

What it is

A truth members recognise the moment they hear it, and one the gym can act on.

What it isn’t

A statistic or a product feature. The figures back it up. The feeling is what we test in member talks.

Why it matters

It is where the brand meets people: the tension they feel, which Sedulo resolves for the member and the gym at once.

SeduloBrand Key05

Benefits | What Sedulo brings

What Sedulo brings

For the member: what it does

  • Counts by itself – every rep, set and rest, straight from the weight stack.
  • Effort per set – how hard each set was, from how your last reps slow down.
  • Finds your bests – a new best is marked the moment you lift it.
  • Says when to go up – the next weight, when your sets say you’re ready.
  • Remembers – the weight and the seat, machine by machine.

For the member: how it feels

  • Seen – progress you can show, and a gym that sees it.
  • Fair – measured against yourself, so every level can win.
  • Together – spots from the people who saw your set.
  • Sure – you know what to do at every machine.

For the gym

  • Members who stay – a reason to come back from the first week.
  • Nobody goes quiet unnoticed – trainers see who trains less than before.
  • A smart floor – the machines you already own, now counting.
  • The floor, measured – busy hours, and the machines that earn their space.

What it is

What the gym and its members get: what the product does, how it feels, and what it brings the gym.

What it isn’t

A spec sheet.

Why it matters

Gyms don’t buy sensors. They buy members who stay, and members stay for how it feels.

SeduloBrand Key08

Brand Personality | How it comes across

Sedulo as a person

Archetype: The Everyman

The Everyman wants everyone to belong. Sedulo is the regular who knows the whole floor: it notices the newcomer’s first full set, cheers the old hand’s new best, and keeps the rules the same for both.

It doesn’t show off and it doesn’t leave anyone out. Its strength is fairness: every member’s effort counts the same way. Next to it, people feel part of the place.

SeduloBrand Key10

Tone of Voice | How it talks

How Sedulo talks

Sedulo’s tone is:

Plain – it sounds like a regular, not a dashboard.Sure – short sentences, no hedging.Warm – it cheers real wins.Fair – it praises effort, not size.Honest – it says so when the sensor missed.

What Sedulo says

  • “New best on the leg press.”
  • “Leo spotted your set.”
  • “Beat last month.”
  • “Two workouts this week. Six weeks in a row.”
  • “Every set counts the same, light or heavy.”
  • “Lift together.”

What Sedulo never says

  • “Unleash your inner beast!”
  • “Crush your goals!”
  • “Only the strongest win.”
  • “You’re on fire!”
  • “No pain, no gain.”
  • “Guaranteed results.”

What it is

How Sedulo sounds: in the app, on the gym screen, on a poster by the machines, in an e-mail to the owner.

What it isn’t

A list of slogans.

Why it matters

The same voice everywhere is what makes many small messages feel like one gym, and one brand.

SeduloBrand Key12

Discriminator | What sets it apart

Sedulo sees effort on the weights floor, with nothing to wear.

Heart-rate systems see the cardio and the classes. Smart circuits see their own machines. Sedulo sits on the weight stack of the machines a gym already owns, and counts every set, and how hard it was, for every member.

The member just lifts. The gym sees its floor.

Effort, as we use the word: how close a set came to the member’s own limit, read from how the last reps slow down.

What it is

The one thing that sets Sedulo apart from everything else a gym could choose.

What it isn’t

A feature list or a price.

Why it matters

If an owner can’t say how we are different from Myzone or a smart circuit, they choose on price, or not at all.

Graphic elements | The tally

Four strokes and a slash, the oldest way to count. In Sedulo it pictures the gym’s week: every stroke is a slice of the goal, and the strokes fill as members add sets.

  • Orange on white, white on orange.
  • Strokes round-ended, all the same width.
  • Never part of the logo.

What it is

The tally mark, and the week drawn as tally strokes: the gym’s shared total.

Why this

Everyone has counted this way. It turns a big number into something a member can see themselves adding to.

What we dropped

The tally as the logo (version 4), and progress rings, which every fitness app already has.
