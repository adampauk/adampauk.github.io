The sensor

A quiet helper on every machine.

It sits on the weight stack and counts when the stack moves. You never have to touch it after the first tap.

  • On the weight stackEvery rep is counted, on the machines your gym already has.
  • Nothing to typeNo buttons, no logging, no counting in your head.
  • No cameraIt measures the stack, never you. No video, ever.
  • Made in a real gymBuilt and tested on the floor of our first gym in Brno.

The app

A calm guide in your pocket.

Before you lift it shows you how. While you lift you can put it away. After the set it tells you how it went.

Questions

Ask anything.

I have never been to a gym. Is this for me?

Yes, it was made for exactly that. Every machine shows you how before you lift.

Will it tell me what weight to use?

It suggests a light start, then adjusts each week from how your sets felt and how evenly you moved.

What if I do something wrong?

It tells you plainly, for example when you rush the reps, and how to fix it next set.

Do I need my phone the whole time?

Only to tap in and to read the guide. While you lift, the sensor counts.

Does it film me?

No. There is no camera. The sensor only measures the weight stack.
