Brand direction, round three

easy does it

Every machine shows you how.

The gym is confusing when you are new. The sensor on every machine shows you how to start, counts for you and tells you plainly how it went, so you are never lost.

Open the websiteOpen the appSee the posts

The idea

Make the gym feel easy from the first day.

What people feel

Walking in for the first time, nobody tells you how heavy, how many, or whether you did it right. About half of people say the gym intimidates them.

What the sensor makes possible

Every machine can explain itself: how to set it up, counting for you, and one plain sentence after each set.

The promise

You will always know what to do next, on every machine.

Who it’s for

Beginners, people coming back after years, and anyone who never felt the gym was for them.

Why it could win

1 in 2 new members leave within six months. The first weeks are where gyms lose people, and this speaks to exactly that moment.HFA and IHRSA figures, cited by Health Club Management, 2024

The risk

It can sound patronising, and experienced lifters may find it soft. So the voice stays adult: plain facts, no cheering, no cartoons.

The name

easy does it

easy does it is what a good coach says when you lift for the first time: no rush, do it right. It tells beginners the gym can be calm, and it is how the sensor wants you to lift, slow and even.
