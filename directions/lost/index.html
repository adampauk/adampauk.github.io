Never lost in the gym.

Every machine explains itself.

Round one: copied as close as possible from Headspace, its website, its app, its e-mails and its feed, characters included, with our sensor and story swapped in. The skills and our own version come next.

The story

Nobody tells you how.

Walk into a gym for the first time and every machine is a question. Where do I sit? How heavy? Am I doing it wrong, and is everybody watching? Most people never ask. Many just stop coming.

readout puts a small sensor on every machine. Tap your phone and the machine explains itself in four short steps. The sensor counts your reps, the app tells you in plain words how the set went, and your first four weeks are planned for you.

Headspace made meditation feel kind and doable for people who thought it was not for them. This direction does the same for the gym floor: warm, round, never patronising.

The enemy

Gymtimidation.

The promise

Every machine, explained. Your first four weeks, planned.
