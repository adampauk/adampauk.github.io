Frequently asked questions

What is Sedulo?

A small sensor on each gym machine and an app on your phone. The sensor counts your reps. The app shows you how to use the machine, what to lift and how the set went. It is a concept in development.

Do I need to know anything about gyms?

No. That is the point. Every machine shows you how to start, in four short steps, and your first four weeks are planned for you.

How does the sensor know my set?

It sits on the machine and senses each movement, so it counts every rep without you touching your phone.

Does it work on every machine?

It is made for the machines gyms already have. The first version is being tried on weight machines at FitPods in Brno.

Can a trainer see my workouts?

Only if you say yes. A trainer at your gym can then follow your first weeks and send your next plan to your phone.

What does it cost?

There is no price yet. Sedulo is a concept in development.

Where can I try it?
