Questions.

Which machines does it work with?

Weight-stack machines today: the leg press, chest press, lat pulldown, seated row and the like. Free weights are not counted yet.

What does a member do at the machine?

Taps their phone on the sensor once, then lifts. The sensor counts the reps and the rest, and the app keeps the weight and the seat setting for next time.

Do members need another app?

Yes, the Sedulo app. Owners told us they would rather not add a second app beside their own, so the pilot tests whether members use it anyway.

Is it only about competing?

No. Most of it is members against their own last month: new bests, streaks and milestones. Challenges with the gym, and between gyms, come on top.

Who sees a member's workouts?

The member decides. Boards and the gym screen show only members who said yes. The gym always sees totals, without names.

What about members without the app?

The machines work as always. Sets are counted only when a member taps in.

What does it cost?

We set prices together with the first pilot gyms. Ask us about a pilot.
