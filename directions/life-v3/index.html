Brand direction, round three

lift for later

Strength is the habit that lasts.

The best reason to lift is the rest of your life. The sensor keeps a long record of your strength, week after week and year after year, so every session adds up to staying strong later.

Open the websiteOpen the appSee the posts

The idea

Strength measured in years, not workouts.

What people feel

Most people train for the next summer, then stop. Nobody shows them the strength they are keeping for later.

What the sensor makes possible

Every rep on every machine is counted, so the app can show the long record: weeks trained, minutes a week, strength kept year after year.

The promise

Your strength, kept for life, and a record that shows it.

Who it’s for

Everyone who thinks about the years ahead, at any age, and the doctors and physios they listen to.

Why it could win

Older members stay longest. In one UK chain, members over 55 stayed 22 months on average, members aged 16 to 24 stayed 15.Health Club Management, member insight round-up

The risk

Longevity is full of hype. We only say what the studies say, with words like “linked to”, never “adds years”.

The name

lift for later

lift for later says what you do and why, in three plain words: you lift now, for the rest of your life. The whole longevity idea without a single buzzword.
