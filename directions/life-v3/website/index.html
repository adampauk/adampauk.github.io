Why strength

Strength is the habit that lasts.

Most people train for the next summer. The research points somewhere longer: strength is one of the habits most linked to a long, healthy life, and one of the easiest to lose without noticing.

Questions

Questions we hear.

Am I too old to start?

Strength training is linked to better health at every age. Start light, and if you have a health condition, ask your doctor first.

Is this about getting big?

No. It is about the strength you keep: getting up, carrying, climbing, keeping up.

How much is enough?

A 2022 review of 16 studies linked 30 to 60 minutes of strength training a week to a lower risk of death. The app counts your minutes toward it.

Do I need my phone while I train?

Only to tap in once. The sensor counts every rep after that.

Does it film me?

No. There is no camera. The sensor only measures the weight stack.
