Questions.

Is Sedulo real?+

The sensor is a working prototype: it counts reps and times the rest on weight-stack machines. We’re building the first 15 this month. The app, the gym screen and the owner view are designed, and parts of the app already run in our test version.

What does it cost me?+

Nothing. Telling us your gym is free and doesn’t commit you to anything.

Who are you?+

Four students in the entrepreneurship minor at Windesheim in Zwolle. Sedulo is our working name.

What happens with my e-mail?+

We use it only to tell you about Sedulo at your gym, or to invite you to try it. Owners hear how many people asked, never who. Reply to any e-mail from us and we delete your answers.
