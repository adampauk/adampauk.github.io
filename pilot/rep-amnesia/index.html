Questions.

Is it real?+

The sensor is a working prototype: it counts reps and times the rest on weight-stack machines. We test the first 15 on real machines in December.

What does it cost me?+

Nothing. It’s free for members, and telling us your gym doesn’t commit you to anything.

Who are you?+

Four students in the entrepreneurship minor at Windesheim in Zwolle. Sedulo is our working name.

What happens with my e-mail?+

We use it only to tell you about Sedulo at your gym, or to invite you to try it. Owners hear how many people asked, never who. Reply to any e-mail from us and we delete your answers.
